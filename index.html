A taste of the good stuff

Three tips you can use tonight.

Small changes, big difference. This is the format every tip follows: do this one thing, get this result.

01 SURVIVAL

Never dig straight down

Stagger two blocks and step across instead. You'll stop dropping into lava and ravines you never saw coming.

02 CAVING

Torches on the right, always

Place every torch on the right wall going in. On the way out, keep the torches on your left and you'll never get lost.

03 MOVEMENT

Sprint-jump everywhere

Holding sprint and jumping is faster than plain running. Free speed the whole game long — build the habit now.

First thing to learn

Read your coordinates

See those numbers on the left of the page? They're Minecraft coordinates. Switch yours on and you'll always know where you are — and how to find your way home.

Java: press F3.   Bedrock: turn on "Show Coordinates" in world settings.
